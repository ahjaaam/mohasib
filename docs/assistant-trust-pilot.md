# Mohasib theory and research pilot

Prepared 3 October 2026

## Purpose

Evaluate whether Mohasib can turn a professional or research question into a concise, readable answer with sources that a reader can open and verify. This pilot tests the product's trust and research experience. It does not test calculations or replace professional review.

The assistant page currently requires sign-in. The question set below is ready, but no live answers have been scored in this pass. Do not present the questions as customer research; they are a representative test set to validate with accountants and researchers.

## Run protocol

For each question:

1. Submit the question exactly as written.
2. Save the complete answer and all source cards/URLs.
3. Open each cited source and check that it supports the sentence attached to it.
4. Check publication/consolidation date, effective date, later amendments, and whether the source is primary or commentary.
5. Mark any material claim that is unsupported, outdated, too broad, or omitted.
6. Have a Moroccan accountant or legal/tax professional review high-impact legal interpretations before publishing them.

Score each answer from 0 to 2 on five measures: correctness against evidence; source support; currency/version handling; directness and completeness; readable professional French. A 2 means it meets the bar, 1 means partial, and 0 means failure. Maximum 10. A publishable answer needs at least 8/10 and no zero for correctness or source support. Any critical legal/tax answer needs human review regardless of score.

Record: question, answer, date/time, model/provider, citations, score by measure, reviewer note, disposition (pass / revise / hold). Do not count a citation merely because it appears in the source panel; it must support a specific claim.

## Minimum answer standard

Each response should answer the actual question first, explain the governing rule in plain professional French, identify the conditions that change the answer, and stop when the evidence runs out. Cite primary texts or the responsible authority for legal obligations. Use professional articles to explain or orient research, not as the sole basis for a legal rule. Show the text's date/version where it matters. Never invent a deadline, rate, threshold, form, or process step. If the source is not current or does not settle the question, say exactly what remains unverified.

Keep answers focused. Use headings only when they help navigation. Avoid emojis, promotional wording, faux certainty, and claims that Mohasib is more accurate than all other AI systems.

## Independently checked source examples

These are source candidates for the pilot, not proof that Mohasib retrieved or used them.

### Fiscal reference

The Ministry of Economy and Finance's publications page lists the Code Général des Impôts 2026, and the direct PDF is hosted on the Ministry domain. Use the relevant article in the PDF for tax answers and check later official updates before answering a date-sensitive question.

- [MEF publications: Code Général des Impôts 2026](https://www.finances.gov.ma/fr/lbc/Pages/publications0.aspx)
- [CGI 2026 PDF hosted by MEF](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/dgi/2025/CGI-2026-FR.pdf)

### Accounting reference

The Ministry's CNC norms page lists the CGNC, CNC opinions, and materials relating to law 9-88. The Ministry's 2007 circular explains that accounting entries should identify their origin, content, allocation, and supporting document reference. That circular is old; use it only as historical/explanatory support and check the current statutory text for the rule.

- [MEF: CNC accounting standards and opinions](https://www.finances.gov.ma/fr/Nos-metiers/Pages/cnc-normes.aspx)
- [MEF: 2007 tax circular PDF](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/dgi/2007/4604_notecirculaire.pdf)
- [MEF: law 44-03 amending law 9-88 PDF](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/depp/2008/6924_loi44_03modifiantetcompletantlaloin_9_88relativeauxobligationscomptablesdescommercants.pdf)

### Company-law reference

The Ministry of Justice hosts a consolidated law 5-96 PDF. Its metadata and amendment notes must be checked against later legislation before treating it as the current consolidated text. It can support research into SARL governance, but the pilot should not publish time-sensitive conclusions from it without checking amendments and the relevant Official Bulletin.

- [Ministry of Justice: law 5-96 consolidated text PDF](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2024/03/26/LA%20SOCIETE%20EN%20NOM%20COLLECTIF-1711463631100.pdf)

### Company-creation reference

OMPIC provides official Trade Register forms, including the negative-certificate and registration forms, and links to the DirectEntreprise electronic service. These establish official entry points, but do not alone verify every step, fee, processing time, or region-specific requirement.

- [OMPIC: Central Trade Register forms](https://www.ompic.ma/en/content/central-trade-register/forms)
- [DirectEntreprise official service](https://directentreprise-guichet.sys.ompic.ma/)

## LinkedIn draft direction

Do not publish an answer as a definitive legal or fiscal opinion. The strongest posts for this product should show the research method: ask a real question, point to the controlling official text, explain one practical interpretation in a few sentences, and link readers to the source. Include the text/version date and a brief note when an older consolidation needs amendment checking. Each draft should link to its Mohasib answer only after that answer passes review.

Draft hook, pending a reviewed answer: “En comptabilité, une écriture ne se résume pas à un montant et à un compte. La règle marocaine exige aussi de pouvoir retracer l’origine du mouvement et sa pièce justificative. Voici le texte à consulter et les points à vérifier avant d’en tirer une conclusion pour votre dossier.”

This is a draft angle, not a final post: its exact wording should be checked against the currently applicable law 9-88 text and a professional reviewer before publication.
