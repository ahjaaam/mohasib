# Mohasib Marketing Design System

Adapted for Mohasib from the Mastercard reference in
[`VoltAgent/awesome-design-md`](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/tree/main/design-md/mastercard).
The reference is used as design guidance, not as brand imitation. Mohasib’s logo,
gold accent, product imagery, copy, and interaction patterns remain its own.

## Atmosphere

- Warm, premium, editorial, and trustworthy.
- Use cream surfaces instead of sterile white for marketing pages.
- Let whitespace create hierarchy; avoid dense decoration around major messages.
- Product UI is the primary visual proof.

## Core colors

- Canvas cream: `#FDFBF6`
- Warm section cream: `#F3F0EE`
- Lifted cream: `#FCFBFA`
- Ink black: `#141413`
- Mohasib gold: `#976224`
- Muted text: `#696969`
- White: `#FFFFFF`, reserved for lifted fields and cards where needed

## Typography

- Keep the existing Jakarta sans family.
- Headlines use medium-to-semibold weights and approximately `-2%` tracking.
- Body text uses regular/medium weights with comfortable `1.4–1.65` line height.
- Uppercase is reserved for small eyebrow labels.

## Shape language

- Primary and secondary marketing CTAs: `6px` radius.
- Major product/media frames: `8px` radius.
- Compact labels: `4–6px` radius.
- Reserve circles and full-pill geometry for status dots, avatars, and controls
  whose shape communicates their function.

## Elevation

- Prefer borders over hard shadows.
- Floating cards use atmospheric shadows such as
  `0 24px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- Avoid tight, directional, high-opacity shadows.

## Homepage rules

- Use an asymmetric two-column hero on desktop and a single-column stack below
  `1024px`.
- Keep the primary CTA ink black with cream text.
- Use gold as an accent, never as a large filled marketing button.
- Alternate canvas cream and lifted cream between major sections.
- Feature imagery and product frames should use restrained `8px` corners.
- Present the execution center as one continuous five-stage accounting pipeline,
  not as a carousel of disconnected feature cards.
- Touch targets must be at least `44px` high.

## Guardrails

- Do not copy Mastercard logos, proprietary fonts, photography, or copy.
- Do not introduce red/yellow Mastercard brand colors.
- Preserve Mohasib’s existing accessibility, responsive behavior, and French copy.
- User-specific design requests override this document.
